# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

These roles describe areas of accountability, not required staffing positions. Depending on project size and skills, one person may cover multiple roles, or a role may be shared across a team.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Design / UX Leads

### Role Summary
Design / UX Leads ensure that project outcomes meet user needs through research-informed, usable, and accessible experiences.

### Responsibilities
- Plan or coordinate user research and synthesize findings
- Create and validate user flows, interaction designs, and accessibility guidance
- Identify usability risks and define experience acceptance criteria
- Partner with the team to resolve design questions during implementation

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive and accessible experiences
- Validate key design decisions with user evidence
- Reduce usability gaps between intended and delivered behavior

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to connect user needs, product outcomes, and priorities
- Collaborate with Developers to assess feasibility and preserve design intent during implementation
- Partner with QA/Testing to validate usability and accessibility criteria
- Share research findings and design decisions with Project Managers and Stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- User research findings and design reviews
- Design specifications and accessibility guidance
- Usability acceptance criteria and feedback

---

## Engineering / Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Engineering / Technical Leads guide technical design and engineering execution, helping the team deliver maintainable solutions and manage technical risk.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture and technical design decisions
- Coordinate technical estimates, implementation sequencing, and code quality
- Identify technical risks, integration points, and dependencies
- Support Developers with design reviews and technical problem solving

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable solutions
- Surface technical trade-offs and risks early
- Keep implementation aligned with agreed product scope

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinate implementation with Developers and support their technical decisions
- Advise Project Managers on dependencies, sequencing, and technical risks
- Work with Product Managers to explain feasibility and scope trade-offs
- Collaborate with QA/Testing on testability and technical quality needs

### Typical Communication
- Technical designs and architecture decisions
- Estimates, dependency updates, and risk escalations
- Code and design reviews

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads make sure teams can reliably measure project outcomes and understand how delivered changes perform.

### Responsibilities
- Translate success metrics into measurement and instrumentation requirements
- Define data quality, event tracking, and reporting needs
- Identify analytics dependencies, limitations, and privacy considerations
- Analyze results and communicate evidence about outcomes

### Goals
- Make project success measurable
- Provide trustworthy, actionable product insights
- Help teams learn from results after delivery

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to define outcome measures and interpret results
- Work with Developers to implement instrumentation and resolve data issues
- Coordinate with Project Managers on measurement dependencies and progress reporting
- Share findings with Stakeholders to inform decisions and follow-up work

### Typical Communication
- Metric definitions and instrumentation plans
- Data quality and analytics readiness updates
- Outcome reports and analysis

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security / Privacy Leads help teams identify and address security and privacy requirements and risks throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security and privacy requirements, risks, and review needs
- Advise on mitigations, secure design, and appropriate data handling
- Coordinate or provide security and privacy reviews
- Escalate unresolved risks through the agreed project and incident processes

### Goals
- Address security and privacy risks early
- Ensure requirements are included in delivery and verification
- Support informed risk decisions and timely escalation

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with Product Managers to incorporate security and privacy requirements into scope
- Advise Developers on secure implementation and data handling
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on security and privacy validation
- Help Project Managers track risks, mitigations, review gates, and escalations
- Inform Stakeholders of material risks and decisions through appropriate channels

### Typical Communication
- Risk assessments and mitigation plans
- Security or privacy review findings
- Readiness decisions and escalations

---

## Release / Operations Leads

### Role Summary
Release / Operations Leads coordinate operational readiness so changes can be deployed, monitored, and supported reliably.

### Responsibilities
- Define deployment, monitoring, and operational readiness needs
- Coordinate release plans, runbooks, and rollback or mitigation plans
- Confirm alerts, ownership, and support handoffs are in place
- Monitor operational outcomes and coordinate follow-up on issues

### Goals
- Make releases predictable and recoverable
- Detect and respond to production issues effectively
- Ensure service ownership and operational readiness are clear

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Align with Project Managers on release timing, dependencies, and communications
- Work with Developers on deployment automation, monitoring, and operational handoffs
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on release readiness and smoke testing
- Keep Stakeholders informed of launch readiness and operational impact

### Typical Communication
- Release checklists and deployment plans
- Operational readiness reviews and runbooks
- Monitoring updates and incident coordination

---

## Customer Support / Success Leads

### Role Summary
Customer Support / Success Leads prepare customer-facing teams for changes and bring customer feedback into project decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Identify customer-impact and support-readiness needs
- Prepare support guidance, FAQs, and escalation paths for releases
- Share customer feedback and recurring issues with the project team
- Help assess customer-facing acceptance scenarios and post-release impact

### Goals
- Enable support teams to assist customers confidently
- Reduce customer disruption during changes
- Ensure customer feedback informs ongoing improvements

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to represent customer needs and feedback
- Coordinate with Project Managers on launch communications and support readiness
- Work with QA/Testing to cover customer-facing scenarios
- Share implementation questions and support trends with Developers
- Provide Stakeholders with customer impact and readiness updates

### Typical Communication
- Support readiness reviews and customer-facing guidance
- Customer feedback summaries and escalation trends
- Launch updates and post-release observations
